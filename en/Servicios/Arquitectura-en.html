Design that builds without surprises

At OVIS A&E we conceive architecture as a discipline where aesthetics meet technical viability. We don't just create beautiful plans; we create precise construction documents that contractors can execute and building departments can approve without delays.

Whether you are planning a single-family home from scratch, a condominium remodel, or a commercial space build-out, our team of architects works in coordination with our engineers to ensure your project is safe, efficient, and complies with the Florida Building Code.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remodel my house without an architect?

▼

If load-bearing walls or the main structure are not modified, it is sometimes possible. However, for construction permits of any scale, architectural plans are mandatory.

Do you handle the permit procedures?

▼

Yes, we offer the complete service of document preparation and responding to reviewer comments (see Permits and Code service).
