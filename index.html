Frequently Asked Questions

We resolve the most common doubts before you write to us.

How long does a proposal take?

We reply to every request as soon as possible during business hours. The formal proposal with scope, deliverables, and costs is sent after the initial review or site visit.

Do you work throughout Florida?

Yes. We serve projects in [Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, and other counties]. For projects outside the state, please check availability.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The initial consultation and the initial budget have no cost or commitment.

Are the plans signed by a licensed engineer?

All our plans and official documents are sealed by a Professional Engineer (PE) licensed in Florida.

Can you resolve work without a permit or with a violation?

Yes, it is one of our specialties. We perform After-the-Fact evaluations and compliance plans to regularize the situation before the building department.

What file formats can I send?

We accept PDF, JPG, PNG, DWG, and Office documents. Maximum size per file: 20 MB.

Do you offer urgent services?

Check availability depending on the case. We prioritize projects with tight deadlines whenever possible.
