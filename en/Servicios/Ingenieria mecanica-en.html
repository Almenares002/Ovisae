Florida's climate demands smart mechanical design

A poorly sized air conditioning system not only generates high energy bills, but also fails to cool adequately and wears out quickly. Our approach is based on the actual calculation of the space's thermal load.

We design mechanical systems that strictly comply with the Florida Building Code (Energy Code). We integrate equipment, ducts, and ventilation with the architectural and electrical design so everything works like clockwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it mandatory to change the AC system in a remodel?

▼

If the remodel alters the volume of the space or adds rooms, it is very likely yes. The code requires that the HVAC system adapt to the new thermal load.

What is Manual J?

▼

It is the industry standard method for calculating the cooling load of a home.
